# pf-rockspec v3: body assemblages and mining overlays

Opt in with `pf_rockspec::v3::Catalogue::embedded()`. The default `Catalogue::embedded()` remains v1, and `embedded_v2()` remains v2. Old JSON, diagnostics, optical formulae, and exports are unchanged. V3 adds the body resolver on top of the same CPU surface evaluator. Generic `Catalogue::realize_prior` refuses v3, because it cannot resolve formation context or an admitted body. No environment flag silently switches versions.

## Scope and evidence

This is the engine's shared material subsystem, for the geological body admission and fracture/render consumers specified in FINAL_PLAN §3. The native inventory join and in-game rendering remain integration work. The included swatches are manufactured catalogue diagnostics, not a seeded planet, a separate teaching application, field observations, or an ore admission job.

All new numerical priors are labelled RS3 with value, unit, plausible support interval, conditions, evidence kind, support scale, and covariance group. RS3 cites the offline design review review and recalled mineralogy/engineering relationships. No web, downloads, spectral library, specimen calibration, or literature extraction was used. Intervals are correlated prior supports, not independent sampling ranges or measured confidence intervals. Exact zero aperture and unit mode closure are model constraints.

## Facies and realization identity

A lithofacies key denotes a family. A realization contains a closed dry-mass mode, grain distribution, porosity, structure, independent fabric, and mechanics. A `MaterialRef` names the family/version, persistent realization ID, and owning body. A caller chooses an explicit compatible realization using formation context and supplies the body's existing admission receipt. `resolve` validates this once; the caller persists `ResolvedBody`. Cloning/fracturing preserves the ref, mode and overlays. `surface_sample` cannot select a realization, create minerals, or apply an overlay.

The 43 previous keys have explicit `.reference` realizations under `declared_reference`. These are intentionally labelled conditional scenarios. They are never a fallback for missing formation evidence. Additional IDs cover granite I/S type with white/pink K-feldspar, A-type white granite, hydrous hornblende versus drier two-pyroxene andesite/dacite, LIP/island shield tholeiite and island cap alkali basalt, flint/white chert, unwelded/welded ignimbrite, and grey/organic-rich shale. Six additional families are pegmatite, skarn, banded iron formation, laterite, mylonite, and gossan. Those have distinct bulk mineralogy and mechanical behavior. Veins, faults, ore grades and alteration styles are not new rock keys.

`FormationContext` retains setting, protolith, metamorphic grade, burial depth and provenance. This arc conditions selection on setting. It does not invent numeric grade/burial response laws; those fields remain recorded inputs for the inventory join. Unsupported/missing setting or realization is an error. `declared_reference` must be explicitly selected by a scenario owner.

Body parameters include K-feldspar inclusion colour, plagioclase An molar fraction, chert variant, welding grade, organic mass fraction and sedimentary maturity/cement/coating fractions. They are resolved with the ID, not adjustable shader knobs. Intermediate values are representable by future admitted catalogue realizations; no unqualified interpolation is implied here. White K-feldspar changes the aggregate prior, not its mineral ID. An jointly changes effective density, refractive index, and an explicitly uncalibrated aggregate prior. Body density is recomputed after An resolution.

Sandstone and arkose reference modes remove v2's biotite addition and account for goethite grain coatings and silica cement as subsets of their existing dry-mass modes. The current intimate optical mixture consumes these constituents; it does not yet solve coated-grain radiative transfer. This corrects inventory semantics without claiming a new coating-optics closure. Other sediment modes are retained.

Welding reduces porosity from 0.25 to 0.08 and sets UCS to 90 MPa versus 35 MPa. The welded realization has 8 mm flow banding and conditional cooling joints. Optical boundary efficiency is 0.025, with glass internal scattering length 20 mm instead of 0.05 mm. The latter explicitly represents fused/healed shard microstructure. The initial boundary-only candidate failed to distinguish welding sufficiently because the old internal scattering still dominated; that failed test is retained in lane evidence. These are uncalibrated process priors, not a tint or a change to the F3 mixing law. The inherited closure's coarse-grain dark bias remains.

## Canonical units and vocabulary

Mineral IDs are pf-rockspec IDs. Composition means dry-solid **mass fractions**, summing to one. Density is kg/m3. Grain/fabric/joint size is metres. UCS, tensile strength and E are Pa; K_IC is Pa sqrt(m); friction is radians. An is molar fraction, not a modal mass fraction. Physical stock belongs to the world ledger, not this catalogue. Intrinsic pores, interparticle pores, resolved voids and water remain separate.

`INVENTORY_MAPPING_V3.md` publishes all producer IDs and the conversion formula. `inventory_volume_to_mass` requires the producer's density for every volume term, records those inputs, and normalizes mass once. Its grain density is the original producer's sum(phi*rho); it is not silently recomputed using different canonical density priors. Generic pyroxene, amphibole, clay and glass labels require explicit supported species choices. The candidates do not assert that all amphiboles are hornblende or all clays are illite. No pf-inventory or pf-fracture source was changed.

## Fabric and mechanics

`Fabric` is a rock-level record, separate from mineral cleavage and actual `joint_sets`. It declares type, resolved spacing, weak-direction/reference tensile and toughness ratios, zero aperture, and penetrative status. Slaty cleavage, schistosity, foliation, gneissic banding and mylonitic fabric are penetrative. They become fresh fabric surfaces when opened, not inherited joints. Shale has penetrative fissile bedding; mudstone has non-fissile bedding with a separate conditional bedding discontinuity. Joint sets no longer encode `fabric_parallel` weakness. Their spacing/persistence/aperture priors vary by mechanical family, with bed thickness, chert/BIF and basalt cooling distinctions.

Mechanical groups have independent tensile/UCS and E/UCS ratios, K_IC and friction. They are dry reference-direction specimen priors. Fabric ratios are separate multipliers for the future mechanical adapter, not a calibrated anisotropic constitutive law. Unconsolidated materials keep intact elastic properties unknown. Salt E = 25 GPa, gypsum = 30 GPa, marble = 58.5 GPa, obsidian = 69.6 GPa and K_IC = 0.8 MPa sqrt(m). Shale friction = 21 degrees (stored in radians). Salt, gypsum and shale have creep=true with conditions; there is no creep-rate law or permission to treat false as proof of no creep. F1 fracture energetics remains owned by the fracture lane.

## Mining records and conservation boundary

Every mineral ID has a `mineral_metadata` record with `mineral_class` and an explicit liberation-grain-size prior. This normalized table extends the existing mineral record without duplicating density/optics/cleavage. Roles are primary catalogue roles, not exhaustive economic classifications. Garnet/beryl can be accessories and resources. Native metals, sulfides, oxides, fluorite/barite, graphite and three bauxite minerals (gibbsite, boehmite, diaspore) are included. Bauxite is an assemblage, not a fictitious single mineral. The default liberation size is a broad 0.01-10 mm support, nominal 0.3 mm; it is not a recovery percentage or orebody-specific measurement. Effective opaque n/k are recalled surrogates, not measured RGB spectra.

`BulkStateV3` wraps the existing BulkState with its original mode and ordered overlays. Alteration kinds include potassic, phyllic, argillic, propylitic and weathering, plus reserved silicification/serpentinization/albitization. Five closed product priors populate the basic alterations. Four illustrative mineralization assemblages cover quartz-sulfide vein fill, disseminated porphyry sulfides, cassiterite greisen fill and gold placer. These are candidates, never automatically applied or mined.

Each applied overlay names its target and admitted source body, emplacement event and stock-transfer receipt. The source must be that body or explicitly hosted by it. A BodyAdmission records body kind, geometry, snapshot, material frame, stock account, material ref and event. Kinds include host, vein, dyke, sill, fault, shear zone, breccia, contact aureole, placer and nodule. Body geometry is a reference to the geology owner, not a generated plane here.

Removed and added constituent fractions are absolute fractions of the same constant dry mass. Their sums must match; removal cannot exceed existing mineral stock. Alteration intensity equals replaced fraction. Mineralization grades annotate the added minerals already inside that same mode. Sequential overlays replay from the original mode on restore. Duplicate overlay IDs or stock-transfer references fail. Validation failures leave the body unchanged. Density and appearance consume the altered mode directly. A fresh face keeps this altered bulk state.

This constant-dry-mass replacement schema is **not a chemical reaction or elemental conservation solver**. Real hydrothermal import/export, water, dissolved phases, changes in total dry mass and extraction ledger transactions are future world-admission responsibilities. A stock-transfer string cannot authenticate itself: the caller must check it against the kernel's admitted event/account records. This library checks consistency, not trust or world ownership. The shader has no overlay API and cannot mint ore.

## Optics and unresolved work

V3 fixes magnetite k from 2.0 to 0.8 (effective n=2.4, F0 0.2131), plagioclase aggregate RGB to [0.74,0.75,0.76], and olivine to [0.36,0.43,0.20]. These affect existing v3 modes where present. v1/v2 remain immutable. All numeric changes, including derived densities and body parameter effects, are exported by `tools/report_v3.py`. Protected family comparisons use the same explicit surface and body IDs, at 2 m footprint; they do not hide changed numbers.

The three plume scenario priors distinguish LIP, ocean-island shield and ocean-island cap. They do not reclassify inventory, choose a lithofacies or invent a producer setting. The inventory owner must supply the distinction before a join can use it. F3 phaneritic optics, F6 spectral calibration, production body admission, kernel stock accounting, fracture adaptation and native Unreal rendering are not claimed.
